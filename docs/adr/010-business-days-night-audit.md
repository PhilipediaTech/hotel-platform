# ADR-010: Business date comes from the business_days table; night audit is a resumable 10-step job

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Source:** Master Specification v3.0, tab 10 (Decision Log)

## Context

Hotels close days, not calendar midnights.

## Decision

Business date comes from the business_days table; night audit is a resumable 10-step job.

## Consequences

See the Architecture and Stack tab (2) for how this is applied.
