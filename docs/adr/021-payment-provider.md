# ADR-021: First payment provider: Paystack or Flutterwave

- **Status:** Open — decide before Sprint 7
- **Source:** Master Specification v3.0, tab 10 (Decision Log)

## Context

Virtual accounts and webhooks are required either way.

## Decision
